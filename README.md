# Thailand, without the rush

A 12-day first-timer itinerary from Delhi: **Bangkok → Chiang Mai → Krabi**, with flights, hotels, a day-by-day plan, food shortlists, a practical section and an interactive budget.

- Dates: 10–21 January 2027
- Fares and room rates sampled on 17 October 2026
- Visa guidance checked against the Royal Thai Embassy, New Delhi

## View it

Open `index.html` directly, or visit:

https://codemonk-007.github.io/thailand-vacation-itinerary/

- All photographs are from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY, CC BY-SA or public-domain licences. Credits are in the page footer and in `images/credits.json`.
